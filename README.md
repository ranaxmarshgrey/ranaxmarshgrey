## 🔨 Projects

### 🔗 ARIA — Cross-Platform OSINT Identity Resolution
> 🥈 **2nd Prize (₹50,000) — CIDECODE 2026, PES University**

A case-centric investigation platform that links accounts across platforms using **behavioural biometric fingerprinting**, **weighted multi-signal fusion** and a per-signal **explainability layer**.

- 4-platform data-collection layer (Reddit, Twitter, GitHub, Instagram) with a 3-source fallback for resilient scraping
- FastAPI backend with JWT/bcrypt auth and per-investigator access control on a 7-table PostgreSQL schema
- Technical basis for an upcoming research publication

`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React` `Docker` `PyTorch Geometric` `SHAP`

---

### 🔥 Wildfire Ignition Risk Modeling
> 🥇 **1st Prize — HACKFEST 2026 (Climate Action)**

ML pipeline that predicts wildfire ignition risk from satellite data, ERA5 weather and California fire history, with an XGBoost model and a PM2.5 smoke prediction component.

`Python` `XGBoost` `scikit-learn` `Pandas` `Jupyter`

---

### 🤖 CRM Insights Agent — Agentic Text-to-SQL & Hybrid RAG

A **LangGraph** agent that routes plain-English business questions to guarded text-to-SQL on PostgreSQL, or to hybrid retrieval (dense embeddings in ChromaDB + BM25) over campaign documents. Answers are citation-grounded, and low-confidence ones escalate to a human.

`Python` `LangGraph` `LangChain` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `ChromaDB`

---

### 🧪 AI Pipeline QA & Evaluation Harness

Automated LLM evaluation scoring accuracy, hallucination rate, latency and cost across prompt versions, wired into **GitHub Actions** to block deploys on regression. Includes Playwright/Postman tests and a Prometheus/Grafana monitoring dashboard.

`Python` `Ragas` `DeepEval` `Playwright` `GitHub Actions` `Grafana`

---

### ⚡ Build-Your-Own-Redis
> [MY-OWN-REDIS](https://github.com/ranaxmarshgrey/MY-OWN-REDIS)

A RESP-compatible in-memory key-value store written from scratch in Go, with TTL expiry, AOF persistence and single-leader replication, benchmarked against real Redis.

`Go` `TCP` `RESP` `Replication`

### 📬 Placement Email Automation Pipeline

Python pipeline that pulls placement emails from Gmail, extracts fields with regex and PDF/DOCX parsing, upserts deduplicated records into Google Sheets, and sends 24-hour deadline reminders.

`Python` `Gmail API` `Sheets API` `gspread`
